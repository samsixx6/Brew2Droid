The .ttf.gz files contain byte-exact upstream TrueType fonts in deterministic
gzip containers. Source URLs, lengths and SHA-256 fingerprints are recorded
in font-provenance.json. Compression does not change the decompressed fonts.
Font files retain their own licenses; they are not relicensed as application code.

Coverage: NeoDunggeunmo supplies all 11,172 modern precomposed Hangul syllables.
Misaki Gothic supplies the complete 6,879-character JIS X 0208 repertoire,
including its Hiragana, Katakana and 6,355 kanji. Later Unicode Kana additions
and supplementary ideographs are outside that Japanese repertoire.

These are portable fallback typefaces, not original handset or game fonts.
No proprietary firmware or game font data is included in these assets.
